Questions

Pressure washing questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Greater Kansas City metro on both sides of State Line, including Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, Shawnee, Leawood, Prairie Village, Mission, Merriam, and Kansas City, Kansas and Missouri.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high pressure and works best on hard surfaces like concrete driveways, patios, and sidewalks. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions that kill algae and mildew, and it's the safe way to clean siding, brick, stucco, and roofs.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Trust Pressure Washing is a registered Kansas LLC (Entity #10083186) and is insured. We're happy to provide proof of insurance.

How much does pressure washing cost?

It depends on the size of the surface, how dirty it is, and whether stains need treatment. Every quote is free. Send the form with a few details or a photo, or give us a call, text, or email, and we'll get you a quote and get you on the schedule.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We clean restaurants, storefronts, sidewalks, parking areas, dumpster pads, and building exteriors, and we can schedule around your business hours.
