Restaurants · Drive-Thrus · Patios

Restaurant pressure washing that works around service.

Restaurants get dirty faster than almost any other property. Grease, food spills, and trash runoff build up on dumpster pads, drive-thru lanes, and back entrances, and a sticky patio or stained entryway is the first thing guests notice.

We pressure wash dumpster pads, drive-thrus, patios, entryways, and sidewalks, and we treat grease-stained concrete so it doesn't keep coming back. We work before you open or after you close, so we're never in the way of your staff or your guests.

Most restaurants do best on a regular schedule. We'll set up weekly, monthly, or quarterly cleanings that fit how busy your location gets.

For restaurants

  • Dumpster pads and grease-stained concrete
  • Drive-thru lanes and pickup areas
  • Outdoor patios and dining areas
  • Entryways, sidewalks, and gum removal
  • Before opening or after close
  • Weekly, monthly, or quarterly plans

Questions

Commercial Pressure Washing FAQ

Do you pressure wash restaurants?

Yes. We clean restaurant dumpster pads, drive-thru lanes, patios, entryways, and grease-stained concrete, before you open or after you close. Many restaurants set up a weekly, monthly, or quarterly schedule.

Can you work outside our business hours?

Yes. We regularly schedule early mornings, evenings, and weekends so we don't get in the way of customers or tenants.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes. We can set up monthly, quarterly, or seasonal cleanings, depending on how much traffic your property gets.

Are you insured?

Yes. We're a registered Kansas LLC and fully insured. We're happy to provide proof of insurance.

What types of properties do you serve?

Retail storefronts, restaurants, offices, apartment and multi-unit buildings, churches, and other commercial properties across the Kansas City metro.
