Design · Install · Takedown

A home that shines, with zero hassle.

Hanging Christmas lights is one of the most dangerous chores of the year: cold weather, slick shingles, and tall ladders. It's also frustrating, between dead bulbs, sagging strands, and lights that never quite line up.

We handle the whole thing. We help you choose a look, install commercial-grade lights neatly along your rooflines, peaks, and anywhere else you want, and make sure everything is working before we leave. After the holidays, we come back and take them down for you.

Spots fill up fast once the weather turns cold, so the earlier you get your quote in, the better your choice of install dates.

What's included

  • Custom design for your home
  • Commercial-grade lights and clips
  • Rooflines, peaks, and gutters
  • Trees, bushes, and walkways (optional)
  • Timer setup
  • Help if something stops working mid-season
  • Takedown after the season
  • Fully insured crew on your roof, not you

Questions

Christmas Light Installation FAQ

When should I book?

As early as possible. Anytime from January through October is a great time to book. Spots start filling up quickly in October, so booking earlier gives you the best choice of install dates.

Do I need to buy my own lights?

No. We supply commercial-grade lights and clips as part of the service.

What if a section stops working?

Let us know and we'll come back and fix it during the season.

When do you take them down?

After the holidays, typically in January. We'll schedule a takedown date with you.
