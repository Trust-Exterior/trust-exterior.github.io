Merriam Exterior Cleaning

Local exterior cleaning for Merriam homes and businesses.

Merriam sits along the I-35 corridor, with established neighborhoods and plenty of businesses around Merriam Town Center and Shawnee Mission Parkway. Road grime and dust from a busy corridor settle on siding and concrete, and shaded lots near Turkey Creek grow mildew.

We clean homes from the roof down: gutters, siding, windows, and then the driveway, walks, and patio, so the whole property looks finished.

Trust Pressure Washing is locally owned by Owen Johnson and Collin Ford. We treat every Merriam property like our own and stand behind every job we do.
